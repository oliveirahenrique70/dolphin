Case study: Ledger

Turning a three-day manual close into a forty-minute review.

The problem

Every month, three people on the finance team opened the same set of spreadsheets and manually matched transactions across four payment providers. A single mismatched row could stall the entire close, and nobody had a way to see where the process stood until it was done.

The approach

I built a reconciliation engine that flags mismatches automatically and ranks them by dollar impact, then paired it with a review screen designed around one question: what needs a human decision right now? Everything else stays out of view until it does.

The result

The monthly close now takes one person about forty minutes. The finance lead described the old process as "closing your eyes and hoping," and the new one as something she can explain to an auditor in a single sentence.
